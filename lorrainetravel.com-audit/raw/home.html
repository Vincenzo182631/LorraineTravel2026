Our Story

From Havana, 1948 — to the world.

John and Luisa Guiteras opened Lorraine Travel in Havana in 1948, when air travel was still a rite of passage and a passport was a promise. In 1960 the family carried the firm — and its standard of care — to Coral Gables, Florida, where the family built one of the largest privately owned travel companies in the state.

Few travel houses survive nearly eighty years. Lorraine Travel did — because we never stopped doing it the old way: by hand, by relationship, by name.

Frequently Asked

Considering a travel advisor?

A few of the questions we hear most often — answered.

Why use a travel advisor instead of Expedia or an online booking site?
A travel advisor works for you, not the supplier. You receive personal expertise, preferred-partner perks (upgrades, breakfast, resort credits), 24/7 advocacy when something goes wrong, and access that public booking engines simply do not have.
Do travel advisors cost more?
In most cases, no. Luxury suppliers pay the advisor — not the traveler. For complex itineraries we charge a modest planning fee that is consistently offset by the value of upgrades, credits, and amenities included.
What makes Lorraine Travel different?
Nearly 80 years of continuous family ownership, Signature Travel Network and ASTA membership, Four Seasons Preferred Partner status, and senior advisors who personally know the hotels, ships, and destinations they recommend.
Where is Lorraine Travel located?
Our offices are at 377 Alhambra Circle, Coral Gables, Florida — serving clients across the United States and worldwide.
